Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Phi Focus is an offline study-time tracker. Your study data (subjects, sessions, to-dos, schedule, wallpapers, settings) stays on your device and is never sent to us. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only outside service it talks to is Google Play, on your phone, when you buy or restore Phi Focus Pro.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, and we never collect your study data. We have no server and no database of users.

Phi Focus stores the following on your device to provide its core functionality:

  • Subject names you create (e.g., "Physics", "Math")
  • Daily time targets you set
  • Study session records (start time, duration, date, entry method)
  • To-dos you create (title, optional notes, optional subject, completion time)
  • Your study schedule (time blocks and their reminders)
  • Wallpaper images you choose, copied into the app's own private storage
  • App preferences (alert sound, vibration, accent colour, theme, layout)
  • Active session state (so the timer survives app restarts and reboots)
  • Whether you are an early supporter (a single yes/no flag, see section 3)

None of the above is sent to us or to anyone else. It lives in a local database and in the app's private files.

2. Data Transmission

Phi Focus does not:

  • Upload your subjects, sessions, to-dos, schedule, notes, wallpapers or settings anywhere
  • Use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, ad network or social SDK
  • Sell or share anything with data brokers
  • Require or offer an account, login or cloud sync

The crash and usage reporting used during closed testing has been removed from the app.

3. Purchases (Phi Focus Pro)

Phi Focus is free. An optional upgrade, Phi Focus Pro (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase), unlocks phone-down mode and a Pro badge. Purchases are made through Google Play Billing:

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card, bank or payment details, your name, or your email address.
  • The app asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether you own Pro. That answer is checked on your device and is not stored or sent to us.
  • The Google Play Billing Library built into the app may send Google technical information about billing operations (for example, whether a purchase request succeeded) so Google can run and improve Play Billing. This contains none of your study data.

Early supporters. If you had Phi Focus before it became free, the app gives you Pro for life. It decides this on your device by comparing when the app was first installed with the date the app became free, and checking that it was installed from Google Play. It stores only a yes/no flag and sends nothing.

Google processes purchase data under its own terms. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. To manage or cancel a subscription, use the Google Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions.

4. Permissions

Phi Focus requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Keeps the stopwatch/pomodoro timer counting while the app is in the background
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows timer progress while a session is active, and schedule reminders
VIBRATE Vibrates the device when a focus or break period ends (if enabled)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores a running timer and your schedule reminders after the phone restarts
com.android.vending.BILLING Lets the app offer and check Phi Focus Pro through Google Play
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Added by the Google Play Billing Library for its own use (section 3). Phi Focus itself makes no network requests.

None of these permissions involve location, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other sensitive data. Choosing a wallpaper uses the Android photo picker, which needs no storage permission: you pick one image, the app copies it into its own private storage, and it never gains access to the rest of your gallery.

5. Data Storage, Backup & Security

Your data is stored locally using Android's Room database and Jetpack DataStore, and wallpapers in the app's private files directory, protected by your device's own security (lock screen, encryption).

If you have turned on Android's own backup (Google One / Google backup) on your phone, Android may include Phi Focus's data in that backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings, is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data exists only on your device (and in your own Android backup, if you use one). You have full control:

  • Delete individual sessions from within the app
  • Clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → Phi Focus → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all stored data

We have no copies of your data and cannot recover it after deletion. Clearing data or uninstalling does not cancel a Pro purchase: it stays tied to your Google account and is restored when you reinstall.

7. Children's Privacy

Phi Focus is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
